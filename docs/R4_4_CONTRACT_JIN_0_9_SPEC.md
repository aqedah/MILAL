## Independent source boundaries

Run S in a separate Python process from raw BHSA 2021. Write its source audit,
manifest and outputs to a fresh directory. Verify the physical manifest and hash.
Then run M in another process from raw BHSA 2021, with no S output path in its
allowlist. Verify M's physical freeze before opening the historical archive.

Each process enforces an exact file-read allowlist, an independent MILAL-module
allowlist and an output-directory write boundary using CPython audit hooks. No
historical target list is supplied. The neutral rule configuration contains lexical
and structural triggers, not Job-specific references or human group labels. The
existing human-free JIN.0.2 relation-rule extractor and raw TF reader are reused
without changing their frozen code. The common utilities have no human registry
imports. Actual source reads and hashes are recorded, including rule/helper code.

BHSA mother/tab/pargr/rela/code are excluded. No native hierarchy comparison is
required in this release; those features remain unread. Input rejection and import
guard tests cover human labels, technical roots, composition input, forbidden files,
human modules and the other audit module.

The orchestration process does not open the stage's historical config/archive
until both independent freezes exist. Post-blind processing verifies source pins,
the exact JIN.0.8 ZIP SHA256, CRC and manifest. It recomputes blind evidence from each
phase's own raw inventory, verifies no blind bytes changed, and only then publishes
historical comparisons and the review packet. Reading raw Bible references is
allowed; only post-blind processing links HSA identities or historical judgments.

## Strict discovery

Inventory every actual Job clause and its clause atoms, words, morphology, PNG,
phrase functions, constituent order, subject and participant surfaces, relative/
ambiguous subordinate markers, infinitives, time/location and domain evidence.
No resolved participant identity is inferred from morphology or surface recurrence.

For each target, evaluate every predecessor in the existing three-clause local
dependency window. Retain only actual linguistic triggers. This is an explicit
local-dependency search, not selection of the nearest mother. In addition, index
all preceding matching verbal forms and formula families across the whole book;
this retains the full possible predecessor universe for the reused P1 bundle
without materializing all four-million-plus pairs. No chapter window is used.

Every retained relation records the existing JIN.0.2 boolean evidence bundle,
trigger list and limitations. Relative constructions and infinitive dependencies
are explicit/local candidates; pronominal and suffix morphology remains compatible
reference evidence, not established coreference. Same clause type alone cannot
support parataxis; differing types do not exclude it. Raw within-clause finite/
infinitive constructions are separately inventoried without a clause-to-clause edge.

Top-level review retains non-explicit-local-daughter candidates with positive raw
frame/domain-opening evidence, repeated main-line configuration, or scope-initial
position. Other clauses retain their complete disposition and evidence in output 05;
no-source-mother alone yields insufficient placement, not a top-level/root claim.
Local dependency evidence is not silently projected to macro hierarchy.

S_SCOPE_INITIAL_CANDIDATE, S_TOP_UNEMBEDDED_CANDIDATE and
S_TOP_PARATACTIC_CANDIDATE remain separate from S_UNIQUE_ROOT_CANDIDATE_POSSIBLE.
An explicit candidate can support a unique-root hypothesis only if its direct
local-dependency candidates cover the rest of the current scope; even then it is
UNADJUDICATED, never selected. A multiple-paratactic hypothesis requires actual
retained paratactic pairs among top-level candidates. S_CONFIG_INSUFFICIENT remains
available. The schema also permits root-plus-parataxis and other explicit outcomes;
unsupported configurations are not fabricated just to populate every schema value.

## Macro discovery

M independently extracts raw speech/formula, temporal/locative, Wayhi-time, WayX
frame, domain-change, explicit subject-introduction/change, addition/proverb,
cessation and terminal observations. Marker inventory contains all raw clause
observations; metadata separately counts clauses with substantive marker flags.
A marker occurrence is not an accepted macro boundary. Clause-local candidates
remain in the onset/closure inventory even where projection is insufficient.

Generic exact ordered multi-clause signatures group repeated verbal forms,
constituent layouts and raw domains into neutral MF identifiers. Participant
surfaces and formula lengths remain separate evidence. All repeated members and
pairwise alternatives are retained; families carry no cycle/discourse names.

Macro containment requires the conjunction of repeated distribution, a nested
onset before the next equivalent onset, internal/onset asymmetry, participant
surface recurrence, domain compatibility and compatible closure evidence. A single
focal formula, span length or prior human relation cannot create a mother.

Candidate coverage until the next equivalent formal onset is descriptive and
explicitly labelled; an open endpoint remains unknown. Higher-frame evidence
requires separated initial/final raw narrative-domain runs, participant recurrence
and verbal recurrence. It records the observed clause lists, without synthesizing
a whole-book span or choosing the first clause as a root. Both frame and insufficient
configuration hypotheses may coexist. The complete permitted configuration schema
is preserved even when the current evidence does not generate every hypothesis.

## Post-blind comparison and review

After both freezes, compare exact clause identities with historical explicit
controls, JIN.0.2 relation cases, JIN.0.3 scope candidates and the strict-unresolved
macro examples. Marker recovery without relation evidence is partial, not full
historical support. Multiple candidate mothers remain alternatives. No-boundary
claims are not proved by marker absence. Missing evidence is not a rejection.

The three historical unresolved hypotactic fixtures are compared against the
HYPOTAXIS family explicitly. Parataxis-only evidence on the same exact clause pair
is S_ALTERNATIVE_SUPPORTED, not confirmation of historical hypotaxis. Generic old
candidate inventories use CANDIDATE_EVIDENCE scope, and explicit local controls
use EXPLICIT_LOCAL_HYPOTAXIS. Every comparison records its expected family.

Historical macro CHILD_OF, SAME_LEVEL, continuation and transition records are
compared without rewriting them. Composition groups are post-blind validation
contexts only, never root/mother candidates. Exact shared anchors in S/M are
crosswalked without merging their analytical layers or root status.

The human packet presents separate S/M candidate/configuration sections and ten
questions, S-Q1–S-Q5 and M-Q1–M-Q5. Canonical review fields are imported; only
UNREVIEWED is prefilled. Raw 2,938-clause data and full pair evidence are appendices,
not 2,938 individual adjudication requests. No score, ranking, cap or winning root
is used to reduce the evidence. The 220 original scope rows remain lossless in the
impact table; subsequent necessity review depends on human top-level adjudication.

## Methodological statements

Top-level/root cardinality is discovered rather than assumed.

The first clause in the analysis scope is not automatically the linguistic root.

Strict clause top-level structure and macro textual top-level structure are separately investigated.

Paratactic top-level units are a valid possible hierarchy outcome.

A technical root is not an analytical root.

A non-textual composition group is not a textual root.

Absence of a supported mother candidate does not by itself establish root status.

Coverage and formula elaboration are evidence of hierarchical force, not automatic root-selection rules.

BOOK_SCOPE_ROOT concerns current Job scope; CORPUS_ABSOLUTE_ROOT is
OUT_OF_SCOPE_FUTURE_RESEARCH. No full-tree synthesis is performed.
