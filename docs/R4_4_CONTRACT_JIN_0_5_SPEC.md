## C — macro anchors versus local dependencies

configured target. The raw first clause is the neutral anchor; no nearest, chapter,
composition or historical opening rule selects it. Every scan is retained in the
inventory; retained=true requires a linguistic trigger or surface resumption candidate.
This flag is not mother support. All local frozen hypo pairs are independently recomputed
and separated; internal or non-head-aligned dependency is excluded from the macro pool.

Macro hypotaxis requires both main-compatible anchors, no relative/infinitive local
explanation, frozen hypo support, explicit subject recurrence, anaphoric or say→Q
embedding evidence, and focal MATCH/PARTIAL. Macro para uses main-compatible anchors
and frozen para support. Surface resumption and cessation/onset coexist as separate
placement candidates. These deliberately conservative rules may yield NO_MACRO_MOTHER_FOUND;
that is a valid result, not proof that a mother cannot exist. Multiple candidates survive.
