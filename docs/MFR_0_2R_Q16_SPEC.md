Deferred atom bindings remain unresolved. Frame correspondence does not establish temporal/locative referential dependence. ## Evidence graph

Raw fact identity comprises source artifact SHA256, explicit BHSA node/edge identity and its observed payload. No similar text is joined. A word observation, phrase observation, native relation or clause feature record is one raw fact, regardless of analytical views. Derived nodes include domains, visibility routes, composite spans and frozen QW witnesses. SB04's SB01 dependency is expanded transitively. OPERATIONAL_DISTINCT records an implemented mechanism rather than asserting statistically or causally independent raw evidence: the empirical SB04 path here is dependent on SB01, has no unique raw contribution, and is shown as shared provenance. Consult qualification basis and ablation, not the status label alone. Missing references, collisions and cycles fail.

Configuration roots include complete consumed clause profiles plus recorded boundary paths. This conservatively records contextual overlap, not a minimal sufficient proof. Disjoint raw closures may be reported as independent chains; intersecting closures are not counted as independent merely because their mechanism names differ. This can understate causal independence. Therefore zero disjoint chains is an audit result under this conservative closure, not a claim that no linguistically independent evidence exists. Dependency closure is not weighted.

Positive binding witnesses with no qualified grammar path remain positive evidence but cannot qualify a relation. Support-only audit views never become decisive positive owners. Domain membership is NEUTRAL; independently observed domain paths are SUPPORT_ONLY under SB09. Unresolved clauses/atoms/frames remain UNRESOLVED. No missing witness is counterevidence.

## Ablation

For each SB01–SB11, remove raw facts whose positive witness consumers belong exclusively to that mechanism. Shared raw facts remain. A support-only alias cannot save a fact from removal. Recompute survival of complete recorded witness closures; preserve every original output. Report lost support, surviving/changed paths, independent-to-corroborative change, unaffected relations and alternative paths. This is a conservative recorded-provenance experiment, not a score or minimal causal proof. Raw-fact ownership is book-wide among recovered positive mechanisms; it is not a test of disabling the mechanism label. Thus a relation can survive unique-evidence ablation even when it has only one directly qualifying mechanism, because that mechanism uses raw facts shared elsewhere. Views that merely share broad context are not automatically independent reasons.
