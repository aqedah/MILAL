1. Core problem
==================================================

Q1.2/Q1.3 currently profile clauses individually.

But some formally meaningful constructions are realized by
MULTIPLE ADJACENT CLAUSES / CLAUSE_ATOMS.

Important example for post-freeze diagnosis:

Job 27:1:

A
ויסף איוב

B
שאת משלו

C
ויאמר

At present these are represented mainly as separate clause profiles.

This can prevent a relation from qualifying even when the
COMPOSITE SURFACE CONSTRUCTION supplies strong pair-specific
source binding.

Q1.4 must therefore distinguish:

CLAUSE PROFILE

from

COMPOSITE SURFACE CONSTRUCTION PROFILE.

==================================================
2. Critical methodological rule
==================================================

A composite construction must be established
INDEPENDENTLY OF THE RELATION EDGE BEING TESTED.

Forbidden circular process:

candidate edge A→B
→ use A→B to define a composite unit
→ use that composite unit to prove A→B.

Required process:

raw BHSA/MILAL surface data
→ independently bounded local construction span
→ composite profile
→ correspondence between two composite profiles
→ source-binding witness
→ existing relation candidate may qualify.

==================================================
4. Independent surface span generation
==================================================

Generate candidate composite spans from observable data only.

Allowed evidence:

A. contiguous clause / clause_atom sequence;

B. native BHSA/ETCBC clause dependency
where the dependency exists independently in the database;

C. explicit subordinating construction;

D. independently established valency binding;

E. explicit direct-speech marker sequence;

F. explicit formula continuation;

G. shared grammatical construction whose components form
a locally bounded sequence;

H. clause-level construction in which a following clause is
syntactically attached to an earlier clause inside the same
local construction;

I. independently observable closure of the local formula.

Forbidden:

- MILAL provisional mother relation under test;
- Q1/Q1.2/Q1.3 inferred relation reachability;
- historical HSA hierarchy;
- chapter boundaries as structural evidence;
- commentary/traditional outline;
- semantic topic alone;
- expected control answer.

14. Conservative positive composite binding
==================================================

A composite comparison may create a positive
COMPOSITE_SOURCE_BINDING witness only when:

A.
both spans are independently bounded

AND

B.
their internal position mapping is independently observable

AND

C.
they have at least one PAIR-SPECIFIC binding chain
that goes beyond bare generic formula repetition.

Allowed pair-specific binding chains:

1.
COMPOSITE_LEXICAL_ROLE_CHAIN

Repeated explicit lexical anchors in corresponding
grammatical positions across the construction.

2.
TEMPORAL_FRAME_CHAIN

Independently matching temporal-frame organization.

3.
LOCATIVE_FRAME_CHAIN

Independently matching locative-frame organization.

4.
PARTICIPANT_ROLE_CHAIN

Explicit participant identities/roles mapped through
multiple construction positions,
provided identity is surface-explicit and not inferred
from PNG alone.

5.
NATIVE_DEPENDENCY_PATTERN_CHAIN

Comparable independently recorded internal dependency
organization PLUS a non-generic lexical/frame anchor.
