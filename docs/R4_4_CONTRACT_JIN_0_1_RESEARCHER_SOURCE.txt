1. 연구자가 승인한 방법론 수정
==================================================

다음 원칙을 HUMAN METHODOLOGICAL DECISION으로 기록한다.

JIN_SINGLE_MOTHER_PRINCIPLE:

“A daughter clause may have multiple mother-clause candidates during
analysis, but in the final textual hierarchy one mother clause is selected.
One mother may have multiple daughters.”

MILAL 적용 원칙:

“Every textual daughter unit that participates in the
TEXTUAL_HIERARCHY layer must ultimately be related to exactly one
direct textual mother, unless it is explicitly adjudicated as the textual
root of the analysis scope.”

중요한 구별:

EVERY MILAL NODE NEEDS A PARENT = FALSE

EVERY TEXTUAL-HIERARCHY DAUGHTER NEEDS ONE MOTHER = TRUE

즉 single-mother principle의 적용 대상은
MILAL 전체 graph의 모든 node가 아니라
TEXTUAL_HIERARCHY layer의 실제 textual node이다.

2. 진규상 원칙의 출처를 문서화
==================================================

Methodological source:

Gyusang Jin,
Investigating the Text-hierarchical Structures and Composition of Numbers,
methodology §3.2.9.

핵심 내용:

- daughter clause may have multiple mother candidates during analysis;
- in the end one mother clause has to be selected;
- each daughter is related to one mother;
- one mother may have multiple daughters;
- candidate selection depends on syntactic/linguistic correspondence;
- this is not a nearest-preceding-clause heuristic.

가능하면 repository의 기존 methodology documentation과
사용자 논문 방법론 문서에 이미 존재하는 Jin summary도 cross-reference한다.
