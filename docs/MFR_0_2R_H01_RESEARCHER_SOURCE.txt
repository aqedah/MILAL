==================================================

Definitions:

TRUE_DECISION_PIVOT:
two or more positively qualified structural assignments
cannot simultaneously be true.

QUALIFIED_NONCOMPETING_CANDIDATE:
one qualified structural relation exists,
but it is NOT human-accepted or canonical.

QUALIFIED_COMPATIBLE_SET:
multiple positive relations coexist under current
structural constraints.

DEFERRED_COMPATIBILITY_UNRESOLVED:
compatibility cannot yet be established,
but no positive incompatibility has been demonstrated.

EVIDENCE_GAP_NOT_STRUCTURAL_DECISION:
reference/valency/domain/etc. remains unresolved,
but no competing positive structural assignments exist.

5. Critical rule
==================================================

HUMAN_ADJUDICATION_REQUIRED only if:

at least two POSITIVE structural commitments
are mutually incompatible

OR

one positive structural commitment conflicts with an
explicit, independently constrained NO_RELATION / EXCLUDED
commitment.

Do NOT create human review merely because:

- evidence is incomplete;
- identity is unresolved;
- one candidate lacks acceptance;
- more than one parallel peer exists;
- mother and parallel relations coexist;
- a target belongs to a variant component;
- source mechanisms disagree only in provenance;
- a relation has several evidence paths.
