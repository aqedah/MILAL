==================================================
0. 연구자의 수정된 방법론 — 최우선
==================================================

MILAL의 발견 순서는 반드시 다음과 같다.

SURFACE-LINGUISTIC MARKERS
        ↓
MARKER FAMILIES
        ↓
FORMAL / SYNTACTIC CORRESPONDENCE
        ↓
HIERARCHICAL-FORCE EVIDENCE
        ↓
MARKER-TO-MARKER RELATION CANDIDATES
        ↓
HUMAN ADJUDICATION
        ↓
TEXT-HIERARCHICAL STRUCTURE
        ↓
PLOT / RHETORICAL / LITERARY INTERPRETATION

절대로 다음 순서로 가지 않는다.

literary outline
→ scene/group/root
→ marker assignment

또한:

BOOK BOUNDARY
≠
ABSOLUTE TEXT-HIERARCHY BOUNDARY

SCOPE BOUNDARY
≠
TEXTUAL BOUNDARY

책/장/절 정보는 metadata다.
candidate generation을 차단하는 규칙이 아니다.

동일한 표층 형식:
≠ 자동 동일 textual level

서로 다른 표층 형식:
≠ 자동 다른 textual level

동일 marker family의 실제 hierarchical force는
다른 marker들과의 관계, adjunct configuration,
분포, coverage, surrounding configuration 등에서 판정된다.

56. Final methodological warning
==================================================

Do not optimize for reproducing previous MILAL structure.

Do not optimize for reproducing Jin’s final Pentateuch hierarchy.

The goal is to reproduce the METHOD:

linguistic marker first,
correspondence second,
hierarchical-force evidence third,
relation candidate fourth,
human hierarchy judgment last.

If blind evidence contradicts previous MILAL structure,
preserve the contradiction.

If blind evidence fails to recover a previous relation,
preserve that failure.

If multiple relations remain possible,
preserve all of them.

If no hierarchy can yet be decided,
do not decide it.

The success condition of MFR.0.1 is not:
“produce a beautiful Job hierarchy.”

The success condition is:
“produce a clean, source-grounded marker-first evidence base
from which the Job hierarchy can later be adjudicated.”
