## Independent surface spans

Reuse Q1.2's clause-profile extractor, formal component shapes and exact node
identities. The new `SurfaceSpans` adapter starts at every observed clause and
follows contiguous words within the same book. Every additional component needs
a database-native annotation with exact dependent/head ownership inside the
local construction and at least one of:

- an infinitive component (native local nonfinite attachment);
- a native typed subordinate relation from the frozen lexicon;
- an explicit speech predicate continuing a construction that already contains
  a speech predicate or infinitive component.

Native NA/Coor does not itself establish subordination. It can retain an exact
database attachment when the independent nonfinite/speech-continuation test
also applies. This does not promote it into an accepted textual mother.
Ambiguous native ownership is UNRESOLVED; an annotation marked as a tested or
provisional relation is DEPENDENT_ON_TESTED_RELATION and cannot qualify binding.

Traversal ends at the first unbound continuation or word gap. No chapter or
verse boundary, fixed window or maximum linguistic span length is used.
Every completed attachment subconstruction remains a candidate, including
overlapping prefixes. Its endpoint is the end of that local subconstruction,
not proof that a final speech/textual unit ends there. Remote closure markers
do not determine onset extent. Candidate anchor positions retain their exact
source component identities and do not select a final mother.

The observational membership table contains only span ID, clause and atom IDs,
membership basis, independence and provenance. It has no accepted parent,
final textual unit or hierarchy level columns.

## Composite profiles and families

Composite profiles preserve ordered component types, lexemes, verbal forms,
phrase functions, explicit subject/complement roles, speech/subordination,
participants, lexical anchors, temporal/locative frames, conjunctions,
native internal dependencies, position roles and component profile IDs.
All native phrase roles also remain in the composite lexical-role evidence,
including `Adju` frames omitted from Q1.2's participant-role subset. An Adju
frame is explicitly `UNRESOLVED_NATIVE_ADJUNCT`; it is not relabeled as a
locative when the frozen source does not provide that classification. Matching
common nouns in such a role may support the generic native-dependency/lexical
chain, with both raw phrase observations preserved.

Family identity is a hash of the observed ordered Q1.2 core shapes and local
dependency pattern. Lexical realization and frame contents remain separate
variant evidence. No corpus-specific family names or expected answers occur
in discovery or qualification code. Families are observational, not levels.

## Position correspondence and conservative binding

Exact-family mapping uses corresponding positions, never identical word nodes.
Cross-family mapping requires corresponding independently observed formal
positions: initial finite/speech, dependent infinitive, following speech,
explicit subordinate, temporal or locative component. Initial positions do not
map to interior positions. All plausible mappings are retained; ambiguity is
UNRESOLVED rather than resolved by a similarity score or nearest distance.

A positive mapping is one-to-one, order-preserving and maps the observed initial
and final components. Unmapped internal components may be independently marked
infinitive/subordinate/frame components. Native paths through such components
are retained explicitly, enabling comparison across different component counts
without merging families or inferring functional equivalence.

Both spans must be independent and disjoint. Positive binding then requires:

- a pair-specific temporal or locative frame witness from the frozen Q1.2
  adapter, retaining both frames, explicit nominal anchor, role, difference and
  provenance; or
- corresponding native dependency paths plus a matching non-generic predicate
  or nonsubject common-noun lexical-role anchor (excluding configured generic
  speech nouns); proper participant names in any grammatical role do not
  supply this extra anchor; or
- a lexical-role chain across distinct construction positions; or
- explicit participant-role occurrences across positions with corresponding
  native paths in a construction that is not merely a bare speech formula.

The multi-position chain is a formal chain requirement, not a scored feature
threshold. Speech lexemes, clause type, speaker name and sequence length alone
do not qualify. A repeated bare speech formula remains nonbinding even if it
names the same speaker. Pronouns/PnG do not supply explicit lexical identity;
all reference identity remains governed by the frozen reference layer.

## Existing grammar and compatibility

Stream the frozen raw candidate/match/crosswalk records by exact pair IDs and
source/target identities. Composite witnesses cannot create a missing raw pair
or license a relation type. Reuse the existing SB06/SB11 `witness` and `qualify`
functions. Configuration correspondence may qualify an already licensed
PARATACTIC candidate; it does not independently prove directional subordinate
attachment. W-A01/W-H01 restrictions and deferred clause-binding exclusions
remain operative.

Keep all baseline outcomes and their original provenance unchanged. Additional
evidence for an already qualified relation is reported in the delta; new
outcomes are added to the derived universe. Retain byte copies of baseline
qualified/outcome/pivot/human tables. Every resulting outcome enters the frozen
Q1.3 `AssignmentModel`: multiple parallel peers and mother-plus-parallel remain
potentially compatible, nonselection remains UNDECIDED, and only incompatible
positive assignment proofs create pivots.
