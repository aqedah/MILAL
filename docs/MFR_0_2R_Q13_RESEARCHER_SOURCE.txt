==================================================

Do NOT assume:

=

Instead distinguish structural dimensions.

At minimum:

A. MOTHER_RELATION_DIMENSION

B. PARALLEL_RELATION_DIMENSION

PARATACTIC:

C. NON_HIERARCHICAL_TYPED_RELATION_DIMENSION

mother slot.

Do not collapse these dimensions.

==================================================
3. Mother exclusivity
==================================================

For a target:

HYPOTACTIC under source A

versus

HYPOTACTIC under source B

are genuine competing mother assignments
if A != B.

Classification:

MOTHER_COMPETITION

These can create a decision pivot.

But:

HYPOTACTIC under A
+
PARATACTIC with B

must NOT automatically be treated as alternatives.

==================================================
4. Parallel relations are additive by default
==================================================

For a target:

PARATACTIC with A

and

PARATACTIC with B

are not automatically competing outcomes.

Default:

PARALLEL_PEERS_ADDITIVE

Both may coexist unless an explicit structural constraint
shows incompatibility.

Do not impose:
ONE_PARALLEL_PEER_PER_TARGET.

==================================================
5. Same-pair relation conflict
==================================================

For the SAME ordered/unordered source-target pair:

A → B HYPOTACTIC

and

A ↔ B PARATACTIC

are mutually incompatible structural assignments
unless a source-grounded rule explicitly distinguishes
different relation layers.

Default:

SAME_PAIR_RELATION_CONFLICT

This may create a true pivot.

==================================================
6. Mother + parallel compatibility
==================================================

For:

target T
HYPOTACTIC under mother M

and

target T
PARATACTIC with peer P

classification should initially be:

POTENTIALLY_COMPATIBLE_ORTHOGONAL_RELATIONS

not:

COMPETING_OUTCOMES.

A clause may simultaneously:
- be daughter of M
- be parallel to another clause at its level

Final sibling compatibility may depend on
whether T and P can occupy the same textual level
or share a compatible mother structure.

Until that is established:

PARALLEL_LEVEL_COMPATIBILITY_UNRESOLVED

Do NOT turn unresolved compatibility into a pivot.

11. True pivot definition
==================================================

A target is VARIANT_DECISION_PIVOT only if
at least two coherent assignments contain
positively incompatible structural commitments.

Examples that ARE pivots:

A.
mother = M1
versus
mother = M2

B.
same source-target pair:
HYPOTACTIC
versus
PARATACTIC

C.
one assignment contains an explicit
NO_RELATION / EXCLUDED structural outcome
while another positively assigns the same relation,
if NO_RELATION is itself constrained and explicit.

D.
global constraints prove two positive assignments
cannot coexist.

Examples that are NOT pivots:

A.
parallel peer P1
versus
parallel peer P2
when both can coexist

B.
mother M
versus
mother M + parallel P

C.
parallel P selected
versus parallel P UNDECIDED

D.
same structural assignment with different rule provenance

E.
same relations with different evidence paths
