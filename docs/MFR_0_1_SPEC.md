Authority: [exact researcher request](MFR_0_1_RESEARCHER_SOURCE.txt).
Start commit: `3ee008dc59bc507dcb77ff14be5cfcea0643d4c5`.
MFR is a new discovery baseline. Prior JIN/HSA artifacts and human decisions remain

## Order and provenance

Surface observation → multi-resolution signatures → marker occurrences → overlapping
formal families → hierarchical-force dimensions → alternative coverage/nested
configurations → marker relations → human adjudication → hierarchy → interpretation.
No root discovery or strict/macro classification occurs in blind modules.

Stage 0 classifies every pinned baseline repository file and the previous release
ZIP. Raw linguistic TF features are OBSERVATION_SAFE. The old standalone I/O module
can be independently inspected as DERIVED_BLIND_SAFE but is not reused. Historical
docs/config/artifacts are POSTBLIND_ONLY. Uncertified legacy executable/fixture
dependencies are CONTAMINATED_OR_UNCLEAR. That conservative quarantine is not a
claim that every old program was actually contaminated. MFR blind modules use only
standard-library and new MFR imports.

Separate Job, Pentateuch, Latter Prophets, death/resumption and Daniel/Ezra child
processes enforce exact file-read and MFR-import allowlists. The raw reader validates
BHSA 2021 metadata, word coverage and explicit source identities; it excludes native
hierarchy features. Actual source paths, hashes, loaded code and config are recorded.
Trusted interpreter/stdlib startup is outside the application input audit. No other
MILAL module is allowed even if preloaded. Unknown fields in synthetic/raw records
fail schema validation.

The coordinator freezes each completed process physically, with a complete file
manifest and code/config hashes. All five freezes precede known-control config load;
historical ZIP contents are loaded after post-blind control lookup. Preflight may
hash the ZIP as an opaque asset but does not interpret its contents. Frozen bytes
are checked again after post-blind work. The complete original upstream ZIP and
its member hashes are included without rewriting historical records.

## Observations, signatures and discovery

Observation rows cover every actual clause, retaining each atom's membership and
canonical atom IDs. Seven signature resolutions apply to both clause and atom
occurrences: exact, lexical-slot, construction, adjunct and sequences of 2/3/4
clauses anchored at the occurrence's clause. Sequence endpoints outside the selected
scope are explicitly incomplete. Atom signatures preserve their own local words;
sequence signatures describe their containing clause's surrounding configuration.

Generic triggers include lexical-slot and multi-clause recurrence, temporal/locative
frames, speech predicates and reported-speech configuration, cessation, explicit
subject shift, known-domain change, add/proverb/speech, open/mouth, be/temporal,
divine speech with explicit subject/addressee evidence, revelation-heading,
death/after-death and first-year constructions. Lexical settings use BHSA raw lexemes.
No reference or traditional section is a trigger. A four-clause construction context
is a transparent formal observation window; it is not an accepted unit or boundary.
Singleton explicit constructions are preserved. Participant surfaces are never
promoted into resolved referential identities.

Local source checks distinguish the nominal death lexeme `MWT/` from the verbal
predicate `MWT[`. After-death requires ordered adjacent content in a raw Time phrase,
not any death verb later in the observation window. Year-one likewise requires an
ordered year/one construction, not a first day and a different year elsewhere.
DSF requires the configured name as a complementary segment's surface head and an
explicit same-clause speaker, or an unexpressed-subject call immediately followed
by explicit speech. A name inside a title/construct chain is not an addressee;
later subjects are not inherited as resolved identities. These general lexical/
configuration distinctions were regression-tested after source inspection exposed
the initial implementation's overly broad co-occurrence tests.

Seven overlapping family types retain their original values, member IDs, occurrence
positions and gaps. They neither merge marker identities nor assign a textual level.
Family IDs are neutral; JM/JMF identify Job occurrences/families. Additional corpus
prefixes identify independent runs only. Adjunct variants are counted by raw BHSA
phrase segmentation and reported with full phrase/word IDs; post-blind disagreement
with a Jin adjunct pattern remains visible rather than being repaired by reference.

## Evidence, coverage and relations

Each marker has a force-evidence row with separate formal elaboration, recurrence,
distribution, contexts, participant/domain, Time/Loca, nesting, closure, coverage,
resumption and cross-family fields. Full family positions and gaps are referenced
from the family registry, not duplicated in every occurrence. There is no score.

Alternative coverage ends include the next exact/construction/expansion occurrence,
an explicit closure, a known-domain break and the tested scope end. Several reasons
for the same endpoint remain distinct. Scope end does not assert textual closure.
Nested evidence uses a lossless half-open interval of the ordered marker inventory:
every marker and all of its family memberships in that range belong to the candidate
span. Counts are computed by exact range queries. This avoids quadratic repeated
listing while preserving all evidence; no marker is discarded for presentation.

Pair indexes use exact/slot/sequence forms, predicate-plus-core configuration,
explicit construction families, death/participant configuration and repeated
surrounding configurations, plus closure-target coverage. All pairs in an indexed
cohort are retained; no cap, nearest-only choice or book/chapter/verse filter exists.
A broad construction match without lexical/contextual corroboration does not by
itself create a pair. Index cohorts are serialized for independent inspection.

Formal correspondence alone is never sufficient for parataxis. Candidate parataxis
requires correspondence or repeated parallel context, comparable surrounding context
and a shared known domain. Hypotaxis is a possibility under overt subordinate form
plus formal/participant and span/context evidence, or temporal recurrence with a
changed formulation. Embedding additionally requires span and participant evidence.
Lexical/participant recurrence and after-death recurrence can produce resumption;
cessation with candidate coverage can produce closure-target/terminal-effect
candidates. These are provisional evidence bundles, not proven syntax or parentage.
Labels may coexist. Different book metadata never excludes any such possibility.
No selected relation or pre-adjudication one-mother rule is used.

## Post-blind comparison and review

Known controls are loaded only after every freeze. They report source-grounded
constructions, adjuncts, families, differences and actual candidate relations.
Jin patterns are supplied by the researcher as descriptive validation references;
this stage does not independently claim to verify Jin's publication or hierarchy.
Original HSA/JIN relations are linked by explicit clause IDs only. Formal recovery
alone is partial support, not confirmation of accepted historical relation type.
Non-recovery is retained without deleting or rejecting the historical judgment.

Historical discovery chronology is classified only where explicitly recorded.
Absent a verified independent-discovery chain it remains PROVENANCE_UNCLEAR and
requires marker-first readjudication. A present-day match cannot establish a past
evidence-first path. STRUCTURE_FIRST_CONTAMINATION is reserved for explicit evidence
that structure preceded discovery; it is never inferred merely from a missing field.

The human packet is organized by family with full machine-readable evidence tables.
Canonical REVIEW_FIELDS are imported only post-blind; the request's additional
family/relation/coverage fields are added without duplicating the canonical schema.
Only review_status=UNREVIEWED is populated. No new judgment, accepted parent/sibling,
root, migration, participant overlay or R4.4 consumer is created.
