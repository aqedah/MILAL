## Evidence and domains

Preserve all 5,854 original witnesses and every original candidate record by
exact-byte copies. Preserve the raw 1,049,504 relation candidates, 243 Q1.4
outcomes, 275 surface spans and 13 human judgments. Native word/atom/clause IDs,
not text similarity, connect every layer. No frozen reference witness is edited.

Domains derive from exact clause-internal grammar, preserved independent Q1.4
spans, and native typed dependency endpoint constructions. Native annotations
must have DATABASE_EXISTING_RELATION and exact unambiguous node membership.
Unspecified NA/Coor sequence alone does not define a new domain. A noncontiguous
native construction contains its observed endpoints, not all intervening clauses.
Two independent composite spans may supply an overlap path through exact shared
clause membership. There is no unrestricted reachability closure of domains.
Domains overlap and nest; none is a final textual or speech unit.

No chapter/verse boundary, distance, nearest rule, score or top-N controls
visibility. Neither tested edges nor historical human structures enter discovery.
Standalone temporal/locative/valency domains are not invented from mere feature
presence. Addressee identity and extended speech scope require explicit evidence;
the present source adapter does not infer either from a generic complement role.

## References, mentions and candidates

Separate suffixes, independent pronouns, deictics and implicit subjects from
proper/common lexical mentions. Lexical repetition remains mention evidence,
not an automatic anaphoric relation. Nominal mentions retain independent IDs;
they are not merged into global entities. The antecedent inventory includes
explicit nominal and unresolved pronominal mentions. A visible unresolved pronoun
remains a competitor and cannot silently be dropped to manufacture uniqueness;
it is not itself promoted into an identified participant chain. Interrogative
forms retain their own type and unresolved status, not lexical recurrence status.

Keep original global search candidates. Add separately labeled supplemental
domain candidates when Q1.2 did not search them, including preceding words inside
the same clause. Candidate counts distinguish these additions; active reduction
does not imply every active candidate belonged to the old global search set.
Forward mentions are not introduced by this anaphoric adapter.

For each structurally available antecedent, record named person, gender, number,
grammatical role, explicitness, speech role, lexical, visibility, native reference
and deictic dimensions. Unknown morphology remains UNRESOLVED. Nominal person NA
may be compatible with third-person reference but is not filled as an observed
third-person value. Common gender is compatible, not an exact match. Implicit
subjects require subject-role evidence; first/second-person compatibility needs
independently marked speaker/addressee roles inside a represented domain.

Every original candidate receives a visibility disposition and remains recoverable.
GLOBAL_SEARCH_ONLY never qualifies binding. Lexical mentions receive no reference
binding merely because another mention shares their lexeme.

## Source binding and identity

A unique visible candidate whose relevant grammar dimensions are supported may
provide provisional SB02 source binding. Identity remains PROVISIONAL_UNIQUE_VISIBLE.
Multiple visible competitors remain explicit and support-only unless independent
exact native word-reference evidence resolves a candidate. Clause dependency is
not native coreference. Unknown compatibility is not silently treated as a match.

SB03 combines independent source-span membership with the target-to-antecedent
visibility path. The antecedent need not be the span opening. Neither membership
nor reference compatibility proves the other. All paths carry domain provenance
and `tested_relation_used_for_domain=false`.

SB10 lexical-anaphoric grammar is not established merely by repetition or definite
form; its output explicitly remains evidence-only in this version. No canonical
coreference graph is created.

Positive binding can qualify only an existing exact raw pair with existing
HYPOTACTIC grammar, following frozen qualifier restrictions. No new relation type
is inferred from reference. W-P/W-H/W-A and deferred restrictions are preserved.
All old outcomes survive unchanged; all derived outcomes enter the frozen Q1.3
model. UNSELECTED remains UNDECIDED; compatible extra relations do not make pivots.
