16. Human adjudication semantics
==================================================

Freeze the distinction:

PARATACTIC
=
a direct configuration-level textual relation is accepted.

FORMAL_ONLY
=
a meaningful linguistic correspondence exists,
but no direct textual-hierarchy relation is asserted.

INSUFFICIENT
=
current evidence is insufficient even for a direct
configuration-level hierarchy relation.

Important:

FORMAL_ONLY
≠
linguistically irrelevant

INSUFFICIENT
≠
NO_RELATION in all analytical dimensions

A FORMAL_ONLY or INSUFFICIENT hierarchy case
may later participate in:

- resumption
- lexical/rhetorical overlay
- local syntax
- other relation dimensions

22. Methodological calibration outcomes
==================================================

Record these as human-approved calibration principles.

CAL-H1-01
Repeated multi-clause configuration can support
PARATACTIC adjudication.

CAL-H1-02
Exact repetition of an isolated internal clause does not
automatically establish a direct paratactic textual edge.

CAL-H1-03
Homologous constituents inside parallel configurations do not
automatically become direct siblings.

CAL-H1-04
Lexical recurrence alone is insufficient for hierarchy.

CAL-H1-05
A strong long-distance lexico-syntactic correspondence may remain
FORMAL_ONLY without a hierarchy edge.

CAL-H1-06
Same repeated onset formula may support parataxis when
multi-clause configuration correspondence is independently recovered
and no formal hypotactic asymmetry is established.

CAL-H1-07
Cessation-derived coverage is not independent evidence for
a hierarchy judgment.
